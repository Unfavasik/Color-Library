Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Color Library. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure, transparent experience while exploring color palettes, CSS gradients, and HTML color codes.

2. Information We Collect

Color Library does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

3. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, no user data is shared, sold, or rented to third parties. Any interactions remain strictly confidential and client-side.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage solely to save your favorite color selections and UI preferences locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

5. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We do not utilize intrusive tracking cookies or external analytics platforms that monitor your personal browsing habits.

6. Data Security

Because your saved favorites and preferences are stored exclusively on your local device, no user data is transmitted or stored on external database servers.

7. Children's Privacy

Color Library is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

8. Your Rights

You retain full control over your local data. You may clear your browser storage at any time to remove all saved favorites and preferences instantly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in our platform. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please feel free to reach out via our Contact / Feedback form.
